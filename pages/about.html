PRE-PRODUCTION

“Every frame is planned before we even arrive on site.”

  • Reviewing your brand tone & goals
  • Studying floor plans, light directions & property flow
  • Building a moodboard & shot list tailored for your marketing needs
  • Planning optimal shoot times (sunrise, golden hour, night)
  • Advising on minor styling & setup to elevate space character

ON-SITE PRODUCTION

We adjust to the rhythm of your resort – not the other way around.

  • We work quietly, efficiently, respecting guest privacy and resort operations
  • Use of natural & cinematic lighting setups
  • Real-time direction for object placement, linens, props, and flow
  • Drone shooting with proper safety & coordination
  • Golden hour & night shots where needed

POST-PRODUCTION

Our editing style is clean, timeless, and brand-consistent.

  • Curated photo selection and professional retouching
  • Unified color tone and composition
  • Delivery in multiple resolutions (web, print, social media)
  • Optional: Cropped sets for booking platforms (OTA-ready)
