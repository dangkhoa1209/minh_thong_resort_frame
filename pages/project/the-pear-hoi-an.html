RESORT

THE PEARL HOI AN

Location Hoi An, Viet Nam

Year 2024

The Pearl Hoi An is located on An Bang beach, one of the most beautiful white sand beaches in the world. All amenities are within reach with a system of 588 hotel rooms, apartments and super beach villas. In particular, The Pearl Hoi An also has an extremely convenient location, only 5km from Hoi An Ancient Town. Put aside the hustle and bustle of life and enjoy wonderful moments of relaxation with an endless journey of discovery at this beautiful beach resort on the shores of the World Heritage Site.
