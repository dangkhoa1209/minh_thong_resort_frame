RESORT & SPA

RENAISSANCE

Location Hoi An, Viet Nam

Year 2024

Renaissance Hoi An Resort & Spa is five-star resort in Hoi An, designed for curious travelers who see every journey as a chance to be inspired by the unexpected. Experience pristine beachfront comfort on Cua Dai Beach. Relax in thoughtfully designed guest rooms-most featuring private balconies or reserve one of our three- to five-bedroom villas with private pools for an unforgettable family or group holiday. Our prime location puts you a short drive from Hoi An Ancient Town, a charming destination where beautiful silk lanterns light up the streets each evening.
